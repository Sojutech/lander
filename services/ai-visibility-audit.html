Questions

What is an AI visibility audit?

It measures whether AI assistants mention, recommend, or cite a business when people ask them buyer questions, and why. It combines a query baseline across the major platforms with a technical review of the pages and signals those platforms read.

How is AI visibility measured?

By running a fixed set of real buyer questions across the major AI platforms in fresh sessions, recording every answer, and running the identical set again later.

Is this the same as search engine optimization (SEO)?

It builds on the same technical foundation and adds the layer AI systems read: entity clarity, content that answers questions directly, and the third-party sources AI engines cite. The same site can rank on Google and be invisible to ChatGPT.
