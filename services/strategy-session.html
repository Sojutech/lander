Technical Strategy Session

You need a senior technical opinion before you commit to a plan.

What You Get

A 90-minute strategy session with a senior engineer, covering whatever technical questions your business or agency is facing. That can be one major decision or several smaller ones: a rebuild quote you can't evaluate, a choice between vendors or platforms, leads that aren't reaching your CRM, a site that's slow or not showing up in search or AI answers, how your stack should evolve, or whether to hire a developer, an agency, or neither.

Before the session, you send whatever context helps: your site, any quotes or proposals, and the questions on your list. We review it beforehand so the time goes to your situation.

In the session, you lay out the problems and we give you guidance, options, and possible solutions for each. You leave confident about how to move forward and with what you need to solve them.

You also receive a written summary of the recommendations, so you can act on them yourself or hand them to anyone.

Price

$750 for a 90-minute session. If you start a retainer within 30 days, the full $750 is credited toward it.

Questions

Is this a sales call?

No. It is paid work with a written output. If a retainer or audit is the right next step, we will say so; if it is not, we will say that too.
