Technical Audits

A Sojutech audit is a measured baseline of your site's technical health, and in the Deep tier, of how AI assistants currently answer questions about your business. It comes with a prioritized plan, and the fee credits toward remediation or a retainer.

The Two Tiers

Technical Audit

The plumbing, measured. Everything a site needs to be readable, fast, and structurally sound.

  • Crawlability, indexing, and site structure review
  • Structured data and entity markup review
  • Core Web Vitals and performance measurement
  • Security headers and infrastructure review
  • Prioritized remediation plan, not just a report

From $2,500

Includes AI baseline

Deep Audit

Everything in the Technical audit, plus a measured baseline of your AI visibility.

  • Everything in the Technical tier
  • AI query baseline across major assistants
  • Entity and citation analysis: where you appear, where competitors do
  • Content structure review for machine readability
  • 90-day re-measure included

From $4,500

The Query Baseline, Explained

We write out the questions your buyers actually ask, run them against the major AI assistants, and record who gets named, who gets cited, and whether you appear at all. That set of results is the baseline. Every fix we recommend traces back to it, and at 90 days we run the same queries again and publish the comparison. It is the difference between "we did AI optimization" and "here is what changed."

Honest Expectations

No guarantees. Nobody can promise rankings, citations, or placement in AI answers. Anyone who does is guessing with your money.

Timelines are real. Citation and answer changes, when they come, typically take 4 to 8 weeks to start appearing after fixes land.

90 days is a checkpoint, not a verdict. The re-measure shows direction and movement. Some queries move fast, some are stubborn, and we report both.

After the Audit: Three Doors

1

Fix

One-time remediation. We fix what the audit found, hand it back, and re-measure at 90 days. Remediation starts at $3,500.

2

An ongoing retainer, advisory or implementation. The audit becomes month zero of a longer engagement.

3

Take It In-House

The plan is written so your own team or developer can execute it. If you want it as a formal build spec, the Spec Pack is $2,500, free with remediation or a retainer.

Audit credit: whichever door you pick within 60 days, the audit fee credits toward the work. The audit is a starting point, not a toll booth.
