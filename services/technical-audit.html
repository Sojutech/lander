Technical Website Audit

Something is wrong with your site and nobody can tell you what. Or it seems fine and you have no way to know.

What You Get

We measure the site's technical health: crawlability and indexing, structured data, performance and Core Web Vitals, security headers and infrastructure, rendering, and whether leads are being captured and traced. You receive a written report with findings ranked by business impact, a roadmap where every item is an implementation-ready ticket, a walkthrough call, and a 90-day re-measure of the same baseline.

Price

From $3,000. Additional office locations and multilingual sites affect the price. Quoted up front.

Questions

What is a technical website audit?

A technical audit measures the parts of a website a visitor never sees: whether search engines and AI crawlers can read it, how fast it loads, whether it is secure, whether its structured data is valid, and whether its forms and calls are tracked. It ends in a prioritized plan.

How long does a technical audit take?

Two to three weeks from access granted to report delivery.

What do you need from us?

Read-only access to the site admin, Google Search Console, and analytics at minimum. Repository, hosting, and Domain Name System (DNS) access deepen the findings. Where access is not granted, those sections are done from the outside and labeled that way.

What happens after the audit?

Every finding is a ticket your team, any developer, or we can execute. If you want us to do the fixes, it is billed hourly and estimated from the tickets. Start an embedded retainer within 30 days and the audit fee credits toward it.
