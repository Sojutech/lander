About Sojutech

Sojutech is a senior technical partner for marketing agencies and growing businesses: the technical leadership a company needs but cannot justify hiring full time. The work covers architecture and stack decisions, infrastructure and performance, development oversight, technical search engine optimization (SEO), and AI visibility.

We embed in the client's operation. For agencies that means their clients work with them, and we do the technical work. For businesses it means a senior technical voice that scales with need instead of headcount.

Background

The background is senior software engineering: production systems, architecture, and technical leadership across teams, vendors, and client relationships. Sojutech puts that experience to work for agencies and growing businesses.
