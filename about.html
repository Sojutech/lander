About Sojutech

Sojutech is a senior technical partner for marketing agencies and growing businesses: the technical leadership a company needs but cannot justify hiring full time. The work covers architecture and stack decisions, infrastructure and performance, development oversight, white-label delivery, technical SEO (search engine optimization), and AI visibility.

We embed in the client's operation. For agencies that means working under their brand: their client relationships, our delivery. For businesses it means a senior technical voice that scales with need instead of headcount.

Background

The background is senior software engineering: production systems, architecture, and technical leadership across teams, vendors, and client relationships. Sojutech puts that experience to work for agencies and growing businesses.

How We Work

We measure before we make claims. We baseline, do the work, re-measure, and report what actually changed, and part of the job is telling clients what not to fix. We do not guarantee rankings or AI citations, because nobody honestly can. We sell measurement instead of magic.

Who We Work With

Marketing agencies first, white-label under their brand: see For Agencies. We also take direct engagements with businesses and founders: see Services and Pricing.
