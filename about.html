About Sojutech

Sojutech exists because of a gap: structured data work was too technical for marketers and not technical enough to interest most developers. Someone had to stand in the middle.

Where This Started

Years before AI assistants made "machine readability" a sales deck phrase, schema and structured data were an unglamorous corner of SEO. Marketers found it too technical, developers found it too marketing-adjacent, and the work landed on whoever could hold both sides at once. For us it landed on the technical side of a national agency environment serving some of the most competitive legal markets in the country, where structured data was not a nice-to-have but a knife fight measured in map packs and rich results. That is where this practice was formed: production systems, competitive stakes, and markup that had to earn its keep.

What That History Teaches About This Moment

The AI visibility moment looks familiar. The same gap has reopened: too technical for marketers, too new and too fuzzy for most engineering teams, and filling up fast with confident people selling certainty they do not have. The same hype pattern, the same vocabulary inflation, and the same person standing in the gap. We have watched this cycle before, which is why we sell measurement instead of magic.
