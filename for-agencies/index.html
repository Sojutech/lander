For Agencies

Your clients have started asking why they are not in ChatGPT's answers. We are how you answer that question: senior technical delivery and AI visibility work, under your brand, on your terms.

The Moment

AI assistants are becoming a place where buying decisions start, and clients have noticed. When a client asks their agency "why aren't we showing up in AI answers," the honest response involves structured data, crawlability, performance, and measurement, which is technical work most agencies do not carry in-house. Hiring for it is slow and expensive. Referring it out risks the relationship. Embedding it is the third option.

The Model

White-label and embedded. You sell the work at your rates, present it under your brand, and keep the client relationship. We do the audits, the fixes, and the ongoing technical delivery behind you. Your clients see your agency doing sharper work; we see interesting problems. That is the whole arrangement.

What an Engagement Looks Like

1

Audit per Client

Each client site gets a measured baseline: technical health, structured data, performance, and how AI systems currently answer questions about the business.

2

Monthly Delivery

Remediation and ongoing technical work delivered on a monthly cadence, in your project management tools if you prefer, always under your brand.

3

Measurement Reporting

Re-measurement against the baseline, reported in plain language you can put in front of a client without translation.

The Economics

GEO and technical add-ons typically add 20 to 30 percent to retainer MRR for the agencies selling them. You resell at your rates; we work from a confidential partner rate card. Your margin is your business, and we never see or set your client pricing.

Boundaries, Stated as Features

We never contact your clients. Not during the engagement, not after it. Your relationships stay yours.

We audit plumbing, never your content strategy. You own what the site says. We own what machines read: markup, structure, performance, and measurement.

No placement promises. Nobody can guarantee rankings or AI citations, and we will not pretend otherwise to your clients. We measure before and after, and the numbers speak.

If you want a technical partner your clients never hear about and never need to, let's talk.
