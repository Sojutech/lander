Audits

An audit is a measured baseline of your site: technical health, structured data, performance, and in the Deep tier, AI visibility. It ends in a prioritized plan.

Technical Audit

We measure the site's technical foundation: everything a site needs to be readable, fast, and structurally sound.

  • Crawlability, indexing, and site structure review
  • Structured data and entity markup review
  • Core Web Vitals and performance measurement
  • Security headers and infrastructure review
  • Prioritized roadmap with implementation-ready tickets
  • 90-day re-measure included

You receive: a written report, a prioritized roadmap with implementation-ready tickets for every item, and a walkthrough call.

From $3,000

Includes AI visibility

Deep Audit

Everything in the Technical audit, plus a measured baseline of your AI visibility, also called generative engine optimization (GEO).

  • Everything in the Technical tier
  • AI query baseline across major assistants
  • Entity and citation analysis: where you appear, where competitors do
  • Content structure review for machine readability
  • Lead capture and tracking review: whether your forms, tracking, and phone lines actually capture and attribute leads
  • 90-day re-measure included

You receive: everything in the Technical audit, plus the full query baseline data, competitor citation analysis, and the 90-day re-measurement report.

From $5,000

Pricing Questions

How much does a technical search engine optimization (SEO) audit cost?

A Technical audit from Sojutech starts at $3,000. A Deep audit, which adds an AI visibility query baseline and a 90-day re-measure, starts at $5,000. Every audit ends with implementation-ready tickets for the full roadmap. Additional office locations and multilingual sites affect the price, and we quote both up front.

How much does a generative engine optimization (GEO) audit cost?

The Deep audit starts at $5,000 and includes the AI visibility query baseline: we run the questions your buyers ask against the major AI assistants, record who appears, and re-measure at 90 days.

What is included in a technical search engine optimization (SEO) audit?

Every Sojutech audit ends in three things: a written report of findings ranked by business impact, a prioritized roadmap where every item is an implementation-ready ticket your team or ours can execute, and a walkthrough call. The Deep audit adds the AI visibility query baseline, competitor citation analysis, and a 90-day re-measurement.

How much does fractional technical leadership, or a fractional CTO, cost?

It depends on whose hands do the work. Advisory retainers, where we direct and your team builds, start at $3,000 per month. Embedded retainers, where we deliver the work, start at $5,500 per month. Both carry a 3-month minimum, and larger portfolios are quoted custom. Either way it costs less than a full-time senior technical hire.

Does the audit fee credit toward later work?

Yes, toward an embedded retainer. Start an embedded retainer within 30 days of the audit and the full audit fee credits toward it.

Do you work with businesses directly, or only with agencies?

Both. Agencies are our primary partners, and we also take on direct engagements with businesses. Listed prices are for direct engagements; agencies should contact us about partner rates.
