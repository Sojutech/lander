Services

Two services, plainly scoped: audits that measure and diagnose, with remediation to fix what they find, and retainers that put senior technical capability in your operation.

Are you an agency? We work white-label with agency partners. Start here.

Audits and Remediation

An audit is a measured baseline of your site: technical health, structured data, performance, and in the Deep tier, AI visibility. It ends in a prioritized plan, not just a report. Remediation is the follow-on fix: we take the plan and execute it, then re-measure at 90 days.

Retainers

Ongoing engagements in two modes, depending on whose hands do the work.

Advisory

We direct, your team builds. Architecture decisions, prioritized roadmaps, vendor and developer oversight, and review of the work as it lands. Right when you have hands but need judgment.

Embedded

We deliver. The same judgment plus the hands: schema and technical SEO work, performance and infrastructure changes, and ongoing technical delivery on a monthly cadence.
