Audits

Our audit tells you where your site currently stands before any recommendation is made: technical health, structured data, performance, and, with the AI Visibility Audit, whether AI assistants like ChatGPT know you exist and what they say about you. In the end, you get a prioritized plan, and every item on it is a ticket a developer can pick up and execute - have your own team handle the fixes, or have us do them for you. Every audit includes a 90-day re-measure so you know what moved.

Agencies get partner pricing on audits and projects for their clients. See our For Agencies page for details.

Technical Audit

We check everything a site needs to be fast, secure, and readable by search engines. We'll also check whether you have reliable tracking for your forms, chats, and phone lines to actually capture and attribute leads. Then we give you direction on what to fix and optimize. Your site should not only be part of your overall online presence, but also a tool that helps you get more leads - we'll help you get there.

  • Crawlability, indexing, and site structure review
  • Structured data and entity markup review
  • Core Web Vitals and performance measurement
  • Security headers and infrastructure review
  • Lead capture and tracking review: whether your forms, tracking, and phone lines actually capture and attribute leads
  • Prioritized roadmap with implementation-ready tickets
  • 90-day re-measure included

You get a written report, a roadmap where every item is a ready-to-execute ticket, a walkthrough call, and a re-measure at 90 days so you know what moved.

We run the questions your potential customers actually ask across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, record what shows up, and come up with a plan built to get you showing up where your competitors do. We run the same query set again after 90 days.

  • Everything in the Technical tier
  • AI query baseline across major assistants
  • Entity and citation analysis: where you appear, where competitors do
  • Content structure review for machine readability
  • 90-day re-measure included

You get everything in the Technical Audit, plus the full query data, a competitor citation analysis, and the 90-day re-measurement report, with the numbers either way.

Pricing Questions

How much does a technical search engine optimization (SEO) audit cost?

A Technical audit from Sojutech starts at $3,500. An AI Visibility audit, which adds an AI visibility query baseline and a 90-day re-measure, starts at $6,000. Every audit ends with implementation-ready tickets for the full roadmap. Additional office locations and multilingual sites affect the price, and we quote both up front.

How much does a generative engine optimization (GEO) audit cost?

The AI Visibility audit starts at $6,000 and includes the AI visibility query baseline: we run the questions your buyers ask against the major AI assistants, record who appears, and re-measure at 90 days.

What is included in a technical search engine optimization (SEO) audit?

Every Sojutech audit ends in three things: a written report of findings ranked by business impact, a prioritized roadmap where every item is an implementation-ready ticket your team or ours can execute, and a walkthrough call. The AI Visibility audit adds the AI visibility query baseline, competitor citation analysis, and a 90-day re-measurement.

How much does fractional technical leadership, or a fractional CTO, cost?

Advisory retainers, a fractional CTO for your business with your team or vendors doing the building, start at $4,000 per month. Embedded retainers add execution by us and start at $7,000 per month. Both carry a three-month minimum, and larger delivery volumes are quoted custom. Either is a fraction of the cost of a full-time CTO.

Does the audit fee credit toward later work?

Yes, toward any retainer. Start an Advisory or Embedded retainer within 30 days of the audit and the full audit fee credits toward it.

Do you work with businesses directly, or only with agencies?

Both. Agencies and businesses pay the same retainer rates. Work on an agency's client sites is priced differently: audits at a partner rate and projects at 15 percent off. See the For Agencies page for details.

What happens after I reach out?

A discovery call within a few days, where we tell you whether an audit, a retainer, or neither is the right fit. For an audit, you get a one-page scope, pay half to schedule, grant read-only access, and receive the report and ticket set two to three weeks later with a walkthrough call. Retainers start on the first of a month after scoping. Nothing is paid on the discovery call.
