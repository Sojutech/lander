Services and Pricing

Audits

An audit is a measured baseline of your site: technical health, structured data, performance, and in the Deep tier, AI visibility. It ends in a prioritized plan, not just a report.

Technical Audit

We measure the technical plumbing: everything a site needs to be readable, fast, and structurally sound.

  • Crawlability, indexing, and site structure review
  • Structured data and entity markup review
  • Core Web Vitals and performance measurement
  • Security headers and infrastructure review
  • Prioritized remediation plan, not just a report

You receive: a written report, a prioritized roadmap with effort estimates, sample fix tickets, and a walkthrough call.

From $2,500

Includes AI visibility

Deep Audit

Everything in the Technical audit, plus a measured baseline of your AI visibility, also called generative engine optimization (GEO).

  • Everything in the Technical tier
  • AI query baseline across major assistants
  • Entity and citation analysis: where you appear, where competitors do
  • Content structure review for machine readability
  • Lead capture and tracking review: whether your forms, tracking, and phone lines actually capture and attribute leads
  • 90-day re-measure included

You receive: everything in the Technical audit, plus the full query baseline data, competitor citation analysis, and the 90-day re-measurement report.

From $4,500

Additional office locations and multilingual sites affect audit pricing. We quote both up front.

Audits cover the entire property. Every Deep audit is re-measured at 90 days, because a single snapshot only tells you where you stood on one day.

Are you an agency? We audit your clients' sites for you at a reduced rate. See our For Agencies page for details.

Pricing Questions

How much does a technical SEO (search engine optimization) audit cost?

A Technical audit from Sojutech starts at $2,500. A Deep audit, which adds an AI visibility query baseline and a 90-day re-measure, starts at $4,500. Additional office locations and multilingual sites affect the price, and we quote both up front.

How much does a GEO (generative engine optimization) audit cost?

The Deep audit starts at $4,500 and includes the AI visibility query baseline: we run the questions your buyers ask against the major AI assistants, record who appears, and re-measure at 90 days.

What is included in a technical SEO audit?

Every Sojutech audit ends in four things: a written report of findings ranked by business impact, a prioritized roadmap with effort estimates, fully specified sample fix tickets your team or ours can execute, and a walkthrough call. The Deep audit adds the AI visibility query baseline, competitor citation analysis, and a 90-day re-measurement.

How much does fractional technical leadership, or a fractional CTO, cost?

It depends on whose hands do the work. Advisory retainers, where we direct and your team builds, start at $2,500 per month. Embedded retainers, where we deliver the work, start at $4,500 per month. Both carry a 3-month minimum, and larger portfolios are quoted custom. Either way it is a fraction of a full-time senior technical hire.

Does the audit fee credit toward later work?

Yes, toward an embedded retainer. Start an embedded retainer within 30 days of the audit and the audit fee credits toward it.

Do you work with businesses directly, or only with agencies?

Both. Agencies are our primary partners, and we also take on direct engagements with businesses and founders. Listed prices are for direct engagements; agencies should contact us about partner rates.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No, and nobody honestly can. We measure before the work, do the work, and re-measure at 90 days. The published numbers are the claim.
