# Fractional Technical Leadership

## For Agencies and Growing Businesses

We embed into your operation as your senior technical expert and strategic advisor, sometimes called a fractional CTO. We provide direction on technical strategy, builds, and integrations so you don't have to figure it out yourself.

We'll help you think clearly, even if you don't hire us.

[Let's Talk](https://sojutech.com/contact)

## What We Do

### [Technical Retainers](https://sojutech.com/services/retainers)

A fractional CTO for your business, every month: strategy, architecture, vendor and developer oversight, and accountability for the results. Add execution when you need the hands as well.

### [CRM Integrations](https://sojutech.com/services/integrations)

We provide the technical work that you need to get done - integrating lead intake to client CRMs, leads routed to the right people, with tracking and links that carry their source.

### [Lead Tracking and Attribution](https://sojutech.com/services/lead-tracking)

Stop wondering where your leads are coming from. We'll help you track every form submission, call, and chat, and trace it to its source, so you can see what's working and what's not.

### [Technical Audits](https://sojutech.com/services)

A measured initial audit of a site's technical health, with every recommendation written as a ticket that a developer can execute. Scoped based on needs, and re-measured after 90 days.

### [AI Visibility](https://sojutech.com/services/ai-visibility-audit)

Generative engine optimization (GEO): we measure how often AI assistants like ChatGPT recommend your client's business (or yours), optimize the technical layer they read, and measure again.

### [Custom Web and App Development](https://sojutech.com/services/development)

Migrations, rebuilds, and deployments for websites and web applications on the stack that you need: WordPress, Astro, headless, or custom full-stack. We handle hosting as well.

[See services in detail](https://sojutech.com/services)

Many will claim to do the same but something will feel off, and you can usually tell within the first few minutes. ### Senior Engineering Background

### Built for Long-Term Partnership
