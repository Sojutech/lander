Pricing Questions

How much does a technical SEO audit cost?

A Technical audit from Sojutech starts at $2,500. A Deep audit, which adds an AI visibility query baseline and a 90-day re-measure, starts at $4,500. Additional office locations and multilingual sites affect the price, and we quote both up front.

How much does an AI visibility (GEO) audit cost?

The Deep audit starts at $4,500 and includes the AI visibility query baseline: we run the questions your buyers ask against the major AI assistants, record who appears, and re-measure at 90 days.

What do retainers cost?

Advisory retainers are $2,500 per month: your team implements under our direction. Embedded retainers start at $4,500 per month: we deliver a defined monthly set. Embedded Growth is $7,000 per month and adds expanded volume and third-party authority work. Multi-property organizations are quoted custom. Every retainer carries a 3-month minimum and starts with an integration month.

What does remediation cost?

One-time remediation of audit findings starts at $3,500. A formal Spec Pack for your own team is $2,500, and it is free with remediation or a retainer.

Does the audit fee credit toward later work?

For direct clients, yes: sign an embedded retainer within 30 days of the audit and the full audit fee credits toward it, spread across the first three months. The credit does not apply to remediation or to partner pricing.

Do agency partners pay these rates?

No. These are direct-client rates. Agency partners work from a separate confidential rate card under a partner agreement. If you are an agency, contact us.

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No, and nobody honestly can. We measure before the work, do the work, and re-measure at 90 days. The published numbers are the claim.
